CREATORBOOST · GAMERADDA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Overview

CreatorBoost is a YouTube creator analytics and SEO platform operated by Gameradda. This policy explains what information CreatorBoost accesses, how it is used, how it may be transmitted, and the choices available to users.

2. Google services used by CreatorBoost

CreatorBoost may use Google OAuth and YouTube APIs when a user chooses to connect a YouTube channel. Depending on the enabled feature and the scopes authorized by the user, CreatorBoost may access channel identity information, channel statistics, video information and YouTube Analytics information needed to provide the connected-channel dashboard and related creator workflows.

CreatorBoost may also use Google keyword-research services for SEO Studio when that provider is enabled. Keyword-research data is used to provide keyword ideas and optimization signals. CreatorBoost may use the Google Ads API to support creator-controlled campaign planning, campaign creation, campaign management and advertising performance reporting. These actions are performed only when explicitly requested by the authorized user.

3. Google user data accessed

  • YouTube channel information: used to identify and display the connected channel.
  • YouTube video information: used to display or organize videos and to support requested Shorts/video workflows.
  • YouTube Analytics information: used to display channel-performance metrics and growth indicators available to CreatorBoost.
  • Keyword-research information: used to provide search-demand, competition, CPC, trend and related SEO signals when the relevant provider is enabled.
  • Google Ads campaign information: used to support authorized campaign planning, campaign management, and advertising performance reporting.

4. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the CreatorBoost features that require it and in the manner described in this policy. CreatorBoost does not sell Google user data and does not use Google user data for unrelated advertising, advertising profiling or data-broker activities.

5. Data sharing and transfers

CreatorBoost does not sell Google user data. Google user data is not shared with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be transmitted to Google's APIs when necessary to provide a requested feature. If a future third-party service is introduced that receives Google user data, the applicable privacy disclosures will be updated before that processing is enabled.

6. Credentials and security

OAuth credentials, authorization codes and access tokens are treated as confidential. CreatorBoost uses them only for Google operations authorized by the user. Users should revoke CreatorBoost's access through their Google Account if they no longer want the application connected.

7. Data retention and deletion

CreatorBoost is designed to minimize retention of Google user data and to use data for the requested operation. If persistent storage of Google user data is introduced or materially changed, this policy will be updated before that feature is made available.

To request deletion of data associated with CreatorBoost, contact support@kgameradda.com. We will review the request and delete applicable data subject to legal, security and operational requirements.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use

CreatorBoost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

CreatorBoost requests only the Google permissions necessary for the enabled functionality. Google user data is not used for serving advertisements, credit-worthiness purposes, lending purposes, or other unrelated purposes. Google user data is not sold.

9. Your choices

You can stop using CreatorBoost at any time and revoke its Google access through your Google Account security settings. You may also contact Gameradda to ask questions about data use or request deletion of applicable stored information.

10. Changes

If CreatorBoost changes how it accesses, uses, stores or shares Google user data, this policy will be updated before or when the relevant production feature is introduced.
