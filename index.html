Application purpose: Gameradda SEO helps gaming YouTube creators plan and optimize video content before publishing. It is a creator SEO tool, not an advertising campaign management platform.

Application purpose

Gameradda SEO provides SEO research and planning tools for creators who publish gaming videos on YouTube. A creator enters a video idea, game or topic and target country; the application can then provide keyword research data and content suggestions that help the creator decide what to publish and how to optimize it.

The application does not sell advertising, run advertising campaigns, or manage ads for third parties.

ABOUT GAMERADDA SEO

What is Gameradda SEO?

Gameradda SEO is a creator-focused SEO application designed for people who publish gaming videos on YouTube. Its purpose is to help a creator plan and optimize video content before publishing.

A creator can enter a video idea, game or topic and select a target country. The application can then use keyword research data to help identify useful search terms and opportunities.

🔎 Discover keywords related to a gaming video idea
📊 Review search volume, competition, CPC and trend information
📝 Generate practical title ideas for gaming videos
🏷️ Generate YouTube tags and relevant hashtags
🎯 Rank opportunities using an internal scoring method

DATA & PRIVACY

Clear information about how the application is used.

Gameradda SEO uses Google services when a creator requests features that require them. Google OAuth is used for requested YouTube-related operations, while Google Ads Keyword Planner data is used for the keyword-research feature. Access is limited to the functionality requested by the creator. The application's privacy policy explains the relevant data handling and user controls.
